Set the parameters of one teaching model so that it draws what one slide needs. You get the model, the lesson, the year group, the intent (what the drawing must show on this slide) and the slide's words. Return only the parameters, as JSON that fits the schema.

Every name, label, number and item comes from the slide's words, the intent or the lesson, written as they are there. Add nothing else. Leave out an optional parameter that none of them decides, so the model's own default applies.

The slide decides each choice, not the default: where a parameter offers options, take the one that matches what the slide shows. Choose the representation and amount of detail that suit the year group.

When the slide asks pupils to find something, use the model's setting that keeps it hidden, and show no answer.

Keep the numbers true to the slide and to each other: parts make their whole, and values sit inside the range and steps you set.
