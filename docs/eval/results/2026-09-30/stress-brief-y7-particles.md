# Stress brief: Year 7, eight objectives, six slides

Given to Chalkie by Greg on 30 Sep 2026. The same brief goes into our eval matrix.

- Subject: Science
- Year: 7
- Slides: 6
- Topic: The particle model: states of matter, changes of state, diffusion and density

Objectives (the lesson must cover all eight):
1. Describe the arrangement and movement of particles in solids, liquids and gases.
2. Explain melting, freezing, evaporation, boiling and condensation in terms of particle energy and forces between particles.
3. Explain diffusion, and why it is faster in gases and at higher temperatures.
4. Calculate density using density = mass ÷ volume, with units.
5. Evaluate the misconception that particles themselves expand, melt or change when a substance is heated.
6. Explain gas pressure in terms of particles colliding with the walls of a container.
7. Use the particle model to explain why a substance's mass stays the same when it changes state.
8. Interpret a heating curve and explain why the temperature stays constant during melting and boiling.

Teacher notes: mixed-ability class. Two pupils have EAL. Include a diagram of particle arrangements and at least one check for understanding.
