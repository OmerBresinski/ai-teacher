system-KS*.template.txt = the v4.12 templates with two lines changed:
- Flow (1.): "picture" leaves the list of what counts as new. A picture is new only when it shows a case, place or stage no earlier slide showed. Two teaching slides never teach the same point, and another view of a taught point goes on that point's slide. A reason involving people names who they were, as a teacher of this age would, by their group or where they came from, never only by a role (attackers, enemies, people).
- Pupil line: a question asks one clear thing. From its words alone, a pupil who knows the idea can tell what it is about, what to do and what answer it wants, and its setup holds only what that answer uses.
