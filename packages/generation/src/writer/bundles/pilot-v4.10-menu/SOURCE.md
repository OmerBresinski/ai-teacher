Pilot writer pack v4.10 (TEACH-110 part ar, 26 Oct 2026). Production's pack (`PILOT_PACK_ID`); v4.9 stays as shipped in `pilot-v4.9-menu` for comparison. Schema, layouts and code are v4.9's (`tierSlides` included).

From a quality judge's 4.5/10 on a real Year 2 Great Fire of London lesson (v4.9): bare commands tacked onto teaching text ("... in 1666. Say the name.", "Look.", "Explain why."), the same content explained on three slides, an exit question asking a quick check's question again, telegraphic lines ("Explain spread; name two ways ..."), weak wrong options.

system-KS*.template.txt = the v4.9 templates with these lines changed (4 in KS1 and KS2, 3 in KS3):
- step 1 (flow): each slide adds what the slides before it have not; a later slide on an idea goes further into it, never back over it. The try-one after a worked example is the same method with new numbers or a new setting.
- the questions paragraph: each question, the exit ticket's included, asks what no earlier question or task asked; practice puts the idea to a new case rather than asking pupils to repeat a slide; a hinge's wrong option stays the belief its layout asks for (named in its why), one a half-learned pupil could truly hold, in the same form and length as the right answer.
- the old "A slide tells pupils what to do as a short command." (the root of the bare commands) is replaced: every line is a complete, natural sentence for the age, with no semicolons at KS1 and KS2; an idea that will not fit takes a roomier fit or two slides rather than a squeezed sentence; a teaching slide ends on its explanation, and what pupils do with it goes in the notes; a question or task is a whole question or instruction. The sentence rule covers slide text, questions and instructions; a hinge option or card label may be a short phrase (KS1 hinges keep 3 options at 5 words).
- KS1 and KS2 only: the invented-scenario example says "imagine", not "suppose".

Not changed: the picture lines. The judged lesson's writer asked for a picture on every teaching slide; its missing pictures came from picture search time-outs, a Commons duplicate and an unfetched proof picture, downstream of the writer.

system-KS*.snapshot.txt = the assembled text (`assembleWriterSystem` + `withFits`), pinned by pilot-v410.test.ts, which also checks the paid Great Fire deck (fixtures/pilot-v410-y2-greatfire.json) for bare trailing commands and semicolons. Regenerate, never edit by hand.
